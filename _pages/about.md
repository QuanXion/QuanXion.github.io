I am **Quanxin Wang**, a junior undergraduate student majoring in **Communication Engineering** at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) (Glasgow College, UESTC) and the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/) (James Watt School of Engineering, UoG). 
I am an undergraduate research assistant in [Prof. Zhao Kang](https://sites.google.com/site/zhaokanghomepage/)'s group, working on graph machine learning especially on understanding the geometric properties of relational data and developing efficient, transferable models for cross-domain graph learning.  
I am currently pursuing a dual B.Eng. degree, expected to graduate in June 2027 and seeking Ph.D. opportunities.

## Research Experience

**CurvPrompt** (Apr 2026 – Present): CurvPrompt fixes geometry under-adaptation in dynamic graph prompting by routing each node–time neighborhood to mixed-curvature Riemannian experts instead of adapting inside one fixed embedding space.

**CurvBench** (Nov 2025 – May 2026): Co-developed a curvature-stratified benchmark evaluating 18 models (GNNs, non-Euclidean GNNs, and GFMs) across 14 datasets. Implemented geometry-aware evaluation across near-zero, positive, and negative curvature regimes, validating that model preferences are regime-specific rather than universally transferable. Built a reproducible evaluation pipeline and open-source repository. [PROJECT PAGE](https://sirbabbage.github.io/CurvBench_HOME/)   [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06397)

**SeqGFM** (Oct 2025 – May 2026): Late-fusion Transformer for cross-domain graph learning, decoupling topology and feature learning with per-node KV caches. Improved few-shot performance on 10 datasets and reduced pre-training time.
