I am **Quanxin Wang**, a junior undergraduate student majoring in **Communication Engineering** at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) (Glasgow College, UESTC) and the [University of Glasgow](https://www.gla.ac.uk/) (James Watt School of Engineering, UoG). 
I am an undergraduate research assistant in [Prof. Zhao Kang](https://sites.google.com/site/zhaokanghomepage/)'s group, working on graph machine learning especially on understanding the geometric properties of relational data and developing efficient, transferable models for cross-domain graph learning.  
I am currently pursuing a dual B.Eng. degree, expected to graduate in June 2027 and seeking Ph.D. opportunities.
